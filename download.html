Download JobPilot AI for Chrome

Free, from the Chrome Web Store. Ready in about a minute.

Add to Chrome for free

1. Add to Chrome

Open JobPilot AI in the Chrome Web Store and press "Add to Chrome". It is free, with no trial and no card.

Chrome Web Store

2. Pin it

Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar and pin JobPilot AI, so it is one click away on every job page.

3. Set it up

Pick the free plan and add your CV: JobPilot fills your profile from it. Autofill works right away; the AI features use your own key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.

Works in Google Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS. Questions? Visit Support, or create an account to sync your profile across devices.

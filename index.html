FAQ

Common questions

Is JobPilot AI really free?

Yes! Autofill, application tracking, and basic AI features are free forever. The Pro plan adds unlimited AI cover letters and field fills for power users. You can also bring your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) for unlimited AI on the free plan.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.sync). We don't have servers, databases, or analytics. Your profile data syncs across your Chrome devices but never touches our infrastructure.

Do I need an OpenAI API key?

Not required! Free users get 3 AI cover letters and 10 AI field fills per month without any key. Pro users get unlimited. If you want unlimited AI on the free plan, you can add your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google in the extension settings.

What ATS platforms does it support?

JobPilot works on 50+ applicant tracking systems including Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, Jobvite, Taleo, BambooHR, and virtually any career page with standard HTML forms.

How is this different from Simplify?

Key differences: (1) BYO API key — you're not locked into our AI quota, (2) zero data collection — we literally can't see your information, (3) open pricing — $4.99/mo vs opaque premium tiers, (4) works offline for basic autofill.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Cancel with one click from the extension settings. No questions asked, no retention flows. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
